## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript, Tailwind 4 + shadcn/ui, Drizzle + Postgres/pgvector (Docker, on the laptop for the demo), Better Auth, Claude API. Full rationale lives in `.planning/research/STACK.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. Responsive web only. A colleague will build the mobile app later against the same backend, so this project ships no native UI.

## Users

Incoming Erasmus+ exchange students arriving at Politehnica University of Timișoara (UPT), Romania: Studies and Traineeships, EU/EEA (KA131) and Non-EU (KA171). They are not Romanian speakers and arrive without UPT credentials.

**Current scope (2026-11-21):** students hosted by the Faculty of Automation and Computers (AC), and AC courses only. Students of other UPT faculties come later; until then the app says so and points them to incoming@upt.ro.

They have two jobs:

- **Before arrival, at home, usually on a laptop:** choose UPT courses that match their home-university courses for the Learning Agreement, and work through the pre-arrival paperwork.
- **In week 1, on campus, usually on a phone:** finish the registration steps, find rooms and buildings, and build a timetable that combines courses from different study years and programmes (AC now; across faculties later).

Students use it on both devices but mostly on phones. Phone use here means the responsive web layout. The native mobile app belongs to a colleague.

## Product Purpose

It brings together sources that are scattered today: PDFs, Romanian-only maps, more than 10 separate faculty timetable subdomains and informal WhatsApp groups. It covers a student's critical first weeks in one English-first tool with four modules: an AI course matcher, a paperwork & legal checklist, a schedule builder that works across years, and a campus map.

The product succeeds when a student enters their home courses and gets ranked UPT matches, each with an explanation, that they can put straight into their Learning Agreement. Success also means they arrive with the right paperwork done in the right order.

## Positioning

It is the only tool that joins UPT's catalogue, faculty timetables and official mobility guides around what an Erasmus student actually does: Learning Agreement → paperwork → personal timetable across faculties. Each faculty publishes only its own group timetables, but Erasmus students take courses across faculties and years, so no existing UPT surface serves them.

## Operating Context

- **Learning Agreement:** the app only prepares drafts (CSV / printable view). The official Online Learning Agreement is signed elsewhere, and the faculty Erasmus coordinator gives final approval.
- **Paperwork:** one-PDF email to incoming@upt.ro, Non-EU visa steps, 3cm × 4cm ID photos, 600 RON cash dorm deposit, registration hours at Rectorate Office 107, key pickup at Căminul 11C or Căminul 8C after hours.
- **Timetables:** odd/even week parity, week ranges, lab subgroups, and UPT room codes (B417 = wing B, floor 4, room 17). The schedule exports as .ics.
- **Live demo deadline:** Sunday 2026-11-22.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Matcher results show ECTS, semester, study year, faculty, programme and English status. These values always come from catalogue data, never from model output.
- Suggestions are never presented as official equivalences. Final approval rests with the faculty coordinator, and their contact is shown.
- Checklist content is curated by hand from official UPT guides. Each item shows its source link, the quoted source text and the date it was verified. None of it is generated by an LLM, because wrong advice harms students.
- The course data is the 2026-27 Erasmus Course Catalogue, Automation and Computers file only for now. The UI labels the source year and states the AC-only scope; other faculties are added later.
- The UI is English only (no Romanian in v1). Students sign up for their own accounts (no UPT SSO). The app scrapes nothing at runtime: all data comes from pre-processed seed files.
- Map points of interest are limited to UPT-owned or partner places.

## Brand Commitments

- **Backed by UPT.** The app may use official UPT naming, branding and the UPT logo. No official logo or brand assets are in the repository yet, so get them from UPT; do not recreate or approximate them.
- **Product name:** Erasmus@UPT.
- **Voice: calm and precise.** Plain, reassuring English, like a good international office: exact deadlines and sources, no slang, no hype. Write for non-native English speakers: short sentences, no idioms, and keep Romanian proper nouns exact (Căminul 11C, fișa disciplinei).

## Product Principles

1. **Sources over assertions.** Every fact about admin rules, deadlines or courses traces back to an official UPT source the student can see.
2. **Suggest, never certify.** The app drafts and recommends. Coordinators and official systems decide, and the UI says so.
3. **One place, organised around the student.** Join data around what the student is doing, not around how UPT is organised. Today that is the AC faculty's data; other faculties join later.
4. **Clear to a newcomer.** Assume the student does not read Romanian, does not know UPT's codes and is under time pressure in an unfamiliar city.
5. **Every flow must work in the demo.** Each module works end to end on seed data. Scope is cut before reliability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Most users are non-native English speakers, so use plain-language copy and spell out terms and codes (KA131, ECTS, room codes) where they appear. Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.
